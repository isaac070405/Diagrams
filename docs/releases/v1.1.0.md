Diagram Workspace now has 48 diagram types, up from 21. This release also adds an icon library, improves several existing editors and fixes accessibility problems.

## Download and open
1. Download `Diagram-Workspace-v1.1.0.zip` below and unzip it.
2. Open `Diagram-Workspace/index.html` in Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari.
3. Keep `pathway.html` and the `icons/` folder beside `index.html`.

## What's new

**27 new diagram types**
- **Methods and measurement:** experimental workflow, well plate labelling, gels and blots, contour plot, belt transect and kite diagram, treemap, radar chart, box plot, bar chart with error bars, clustered heatmap, Venn diagram, scatter plot with regression, Sankey diagram and PCA plot.
- **Evidence and clinical (new home page group):** PRISMA 2020 flow diagram, forest plot, Kaplan-Meier survival curve, CONSORT flow diagram, ROC curve, Bland-Altman plot and funnel plot with Egger's test.
- **Cells, measurement and ecology:** volcano plot, enzyme kinetics, standard curve, pie and donut chart, rarefaction and species accumulation, and growth curve.

**Icon library**
- 80 BioRender icons in 7 groups, available on the home page and inside Biological pathway.
- You can add your own icons and groups. Credits are in `site/icons/CREDITS.md`.

**Improvements to existing editors**
- **Histological image:** stain and colour sliders, multi-panel layouts, a calibrated grid, crop and rotate presets, a spline contour and an intensity profile.
- **Gradient bar:** a line-only style, a colour-stop editor and four more templates.
- **Food web:** curved arrows, path highlighting on hover, a hydrothermal vent template, accordions, themes, a link linter, a snap grid and crossing reduction.
- **Biological pathway:** a sidebar ordered by workflow, a Blank Template, more typefaces and text colours, and a typeface and colour for each node.
- **Every editor:** you can now copy the diagram as PNG or SVG.

**Project files and tests**
- New community files: ACCESSIBILITY, CHANGELOG, CITATION, CODE_OF_CONDUCT, CONTRIBUTING, LICENSE, SECURITY, SUPPORT and THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.
- Tests now cover the statistics behind the new diagrams, including exact reference values (`source/tests/`).

## What changed
- The Scale bar diagram is now called Histological image.
- Biological pathway no longer includes the first-draft bridge.
- The CI build job now builds the site as well.

## Fixes
- Accessibility: touch targets are 44 px, pages have main landmarks, chips have roles, focus rules are corrected, and series, steps and pathway links work from the keyboard.
- Downloads now fall back to the browser when the Claude downloads capability is missing.
