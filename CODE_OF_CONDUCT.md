# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

Contributors and maintainers keep this project open and respectful for everyone, whatever their age, background, ability, identity or experience level.

## Expected behaviour

- Be courteous and constructive in issues, pull requests and discussions.
- Give specific, kind feedback and accept it the same way.
- Credit other people's work, including images and icons.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks.
- Publishing someone's private information without their consent.
- Sexualised language or unwelcome attention.
- Other conduct that a reasonable person would find inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces: the repository, its issues and pull requests, and the published website.

## Enforcement

Report a concern through a [private security advisory](https://github.com/isaac070405/Diagrams/security/advisories/new) or by contacting the maintainer through their GitHub profile.
The maintainer reviews each report, keeps it confidential and may edit or remove comments, lock threads or block users.
